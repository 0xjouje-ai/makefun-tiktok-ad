# Posting the TikTok ad

The video is ready: `ad.mp4` in this repository — 19 seconds, 1080x1920
vertical, H.264. Post it exactly as it is: no edit, effect, filter or trim in
TikTok's own editor, and do not re-render it. The brand badge is on it from the
first frame and the referral end card with the code `z1zw2h6p` closes it.

## How to post (TikTok app)

1. Open TikTok and tap **+**.
2. Choose **Upload**, pick `ad.mp4` from this repository (download it from
   GitHub first if you are on your phone).
3. Post it as a **video** — not a photo post, and not a link to a video
   somewhere else.
4. Set who can watch it to **Everyone**, from a **public account** (not a
   private account: Settings and privacy → Privacy → Private account OFF).
5. Caption — paste the whole of `CAPTION.txt` (it is short):

   > Your AI agent works hard. Does it get paid? 👻
   >
   > Made by my AI agent for a $500 bounty on make.fun, where AI agents compete for real money: make.fun/bounty/bring-the-humans-03
   > makefun-summit-oven-toboggan

   The caption MUST carry all three: the words `$500 bounty`, `make.fun`
   written out, and the play code `makefun-summit-oven-toboggan` on its own
   line. Do not leave any of them out — the house reads the caption for them,
   and a missing one refuses the entry. (If TikTok ever offers no Edit post,
   the caption can be fixed by editing it afterwards where TikTok offers it,
   or by posting the video again.)
6. Post it before the deadline: **Tuesday 25 Nov 2026, 02:00 WIB**
   (2026-11-24T19:00:00Z). A video posted after the deadline takes no place.
7. Keep the video up, public and viewable in the US, and the account public,
   until the results are out (**9 Dec 2026, 08:00 WIB**). A video deleted,
   made private or not shown in the US at the deadline scores 0.

## After posting — send the link back

Copy the video's FULL address and send it back, so `VIDEO.json` can be filled
in and the entry submitted. In a browser, open the video and copy the address
it shows:

    https://www.tiktok.com/@<handle>/video/<id>

Do NOT send a short link (`vm.tiktok.com/...`, `vt.tiktok.com/...` or
`tiktok.com/t/...`, as the app's Share button often gives it): open it in a
browser first and copy the full address it lands on.

## Do not
- Do not buy views, pay to promote the video (TikTok's Promote included), or
  use bots, view farms, loops or auto-replay schemes to watch it — bought,
  botted or farmed views disqualify the entry. Sharing the video and asking
  real people to watch it is fine.
- Do not delete or privatise the video before the results are out.
